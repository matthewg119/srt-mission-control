# POV Prompt Library

9 POV workflows · 2 sessions with approved prompts · 45 unique prompts.
Regenerate anytime: `bunx tsx scripts/dump-pov-prompts.ts`.

**How to curate (the feedback loop):** mark keepers, delete duds, and note WHY next to each.
Feed the keepers back as (a) workflow scene prompts in the editor, (b) visual rules (bans and
musts), and (c) reference images pasted into the workflow's Slack session. Every image you
paste in a session is saved as a reference automatically, so the library grounds itself over time.

## 1. POV workflow seed prompts (the library's structure)

### Wasp / Hornet Nest Removal (POV)  (`pest_control__pov__wasp_nest`, active)

Visual rules:
1. Every frame is first-person POV through Ray-Ban Meta glasses; gloved hands may enter frame, never a face.
2. Real suburban job-site settings, natural daylight, documentary look; no studio lighting.
3. No text, captions, logos, or watermarks inside the image.
4. Frame like the native Ray-Ban Meta 3:4 portrait capture: eye-height, gaze-centered, subject filling a tall frame.

**Shot 1 — reveal the nest (open loop)**
```
First-person POV through Ray-Ban Meta glasses looking up at a large grey paper wasp nest tucked under a home's roof eave, wasps crawling over it in the afternoon sun, gloved hand entering frame from below
```
motion: Slow tilt up the eave to settle on the nest as a few wasps lift off.

**Shot 2 — climb the ladder**
```
First-person POV climbing an aluminium extension ladder toward the roofline of an older suburban home, gloved hands on the rails, nest visible ahead near the gutter
```
motion: Hands pull up rung by rung, the nest growing closer in frame.

**Shot 3 — bag and remove the nest**
```
First-person POV sliding a thick contractor bag up and over the wasp nest at the eave, gloved hands sealing it off, wasps scattering
```
motion: The bag sweeps up over the nest and cinches closed in one motion.

**Shot 4 — clean result**
```
First-person POV of the now-bare eave where the nest was, clean stucco, sealed bag held up in a gloved hand against a blue sky
```
motion: Camera settles on the clean eave, then the bagged nest lifts into frame.


### 3 B-roll META POV Pest Control  (`pest_owner_ai__pov__local_owner_pain_to_cta_hook`, active)

Visual rules:
1. Every frame is first-person POV through Ray-Ban Meta glasses DOING pest-control work; the camera is the technician's eyes on the job.
2. The subject is always the WORK itself: gloved hands, sprayer wand, baseboards, attic corners, eaves, foundation/perimeter lines, bait stations, visible treatment.
3. Each of the 3 shots is a DIFFERENT angle or job area (low at a baseboard, looking up at an eave, walking the perimeter) - vary the angle and the job, never the subject matter.
4. No faces and no full human figures anywhere; at most gloved hands and forearms entering the frame.
5. BANNED subjects: truck drivers, vehicle interiors or cabs, steering wheels, dashboards, offices, desks, phones, customers, or any non-pest-control subject.
6. No text, captions, logos, or watermarks inside the image.
7. Real suburban job-site settings, natural daylight, handheld documentary look; no studio lighting.
8. Frame like the native Ray-Ban Meta 3:4 portrait capture: eye-height, gaze-centered, subject filling a tall frame.

**Shot 1 — Avatar callout / baseboard treatment**
```
First-person Meta-glasses POV looking down at eye-height toward a scuffed white baseboard running along the bottom of a beige drywall living room wall, a latex-gloved right hand entering from the bottom of the frame gripping a stainless B&G extension wand, the narrow fan-tip nozzle held two inches from the baseboard joint and trailing a fine mist of treatment solution along the seam where wall meets laminate flooring, afternoon window light from the left casting a soft diagonal shadow across the baseboard, dust and minor paint chips visible at the wall base adding authentic wear, handheld documentary steadiness with slight natural drift, photorealistic 3:4 portrait framing, no faces no text no logos.
```
